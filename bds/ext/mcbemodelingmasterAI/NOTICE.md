## What is and is not in this repository

- **No Minecraft assets.** No vanilla model, texture, animation or code is included. Where a document describes how a vanilla entity is built (the Ender Dragon's wings in `docs/07`), it describes the *technique* in our own words; to see the original, open the vanilla resource pack that Mojang publishes (`github.com/Mojang/bedrock-samples`) under its own terms.
- **No assets of the project these lessons come from.** The karts, the watch and the charm named in the case studies belong to a private game project and are not here: not their models, textures, renders or source. The case studies record what was learned, in words.
- **The two example models (*Comet*, *Drake*) are original**, generated by the scripts in `examples/`, and are covered by this repository's licence, as are the images in `docs/images/`, which are renders of those two models.

## Accuracy

The engine facts here (the x mirror, the rotation convention, the face mapping) were established by experiment against the 2026 releases of the game, not from official documentation. They may change. If the game disagrees with this repository, the game is right — please open an issue.
