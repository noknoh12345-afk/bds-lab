# geoui_probe（bds/geoui/timeline/probe.mjs が作る）: GeoUI の作りの確かめ（ボーンの番号・自分の render controller・時間）。node bds/geoui/timeline/probe.mjs read <summary.json> が読む。
# 基準は何も出さない画面（world--probe-off）。テストを 1 つずつ出して写し、measure diff で変わった所（px）を数で出す。

section title
launch
until title 600000
shot title

section join
join
until joined 300000
wait 3000
do time set noon
do gamerule dodaylightcycle false
do weather clear
do tp @a 0 -60 0 0 90
do hud @a hide paperdoll
do hud @a hide touch_controls
do hud @a hide hotbar
do hud @a hide crosshair
do js for (const q of world.getAllPlayers()) q.setProperty('gprobe:v2', 0); 'probe off'
wait 6000
shot world--probe-off
absent clientlog \bERROR\b

section t01-ctl
do js for (const q of world.getAllPlayers()) q.setProperty('gprobe:v2', 1); 'probe 1'
wait 1500
shot t01-ctl
measure diff t01-ctl
