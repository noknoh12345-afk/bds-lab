# geoui_html_demo（bds/geoui/html/convert.mjs --sample が作る）を本物のアプリで: ページの見た目・焦点・ポインタ・ページの切り替え
section title
stop
launch
until title 600000
shot title

section join
join
until joined 900000
do tp @a 0 -60 0 0 90
do gamemode adventure @a
wait 6000
shot world
absent clientlog \bERROR\b

section open
do scriptevent ghd:open
wait 2500
shot page-play
changed 0.2
measure diff page-play

section keys
press D 300
wait 800
press S 300
wait 800
shot keys-focus
press SPACE 200
wait 800
until server GEOUI event 5000

section tabs
press 2 200
until server GEOUI page 5000
wait 1500
shot page-items
press 3 200
until server GEOUI page 5000
wait 1500
shot page-settings
press S 300
wait 600
press SPACE 200
until server GEOUI change 5000
wait 1000
shot settings-toggle

section pointer
press 1 200
until server GEOUI page 5000
wait 1200
look left 400 50
wait 800
shot pointer
look right 600 50
wait 800
shot pointer-2

section sizes
size 1024x768
wait 9000
shot page-4x3
size 2400x1080
wait 9000
shot page-20x9
size reset
wait 6000

section close
press SHIFT_LEFT 300
wait 600
press SHIFT_LEFT 300
wait 1500
shot closed
