# geoui_rhythm を本物のアプリで: 再生して、決まった時刻に写す（GeoUI のタイムラインが描く: 円・接近の輪・番号・スライダーの玉・スピナー・点数）
# 時刻は再生を始めてからの目安（サーバーの命令が届くまでの遅れがある）。比べる基準は再生前の画面（world--before-play）。

section title
launch
until title 600000
shot title

section join
join
until joined 300000
wait 3000
do time set noon
do gamerule dodaylightcycle false
do weather clear
do tp @a 0 -60 0 0 90
do hud @a hide paperdoll
do hud @a hide touch_controls
do hud @a hide hotbar
do hud @a hide crosshair
wait 6000
shot world--before-play
absent clientlog \bERROR\b

section play
do scriptevent geoui:play 1
wait 1000
shot play-1s
measure diff play-1s
wait 1000
shot play-2s
measure diff play-2s
wait 1000
shot play-3s
measure diff play-3s
wait 1000
shot play-4s
measure diff play-4s
wait 1000
shot play-5s
measure diff play-5s
wait 1500
shot play-6_5s
measure diff play-6_5s
wait 1500
shot play-8s
measure diff play-8s
wait 1500
shot play-9_5s
measure diff play-9_5s
wait 1500
shot play-11s
measure diff play-11s
wait 1500
shot play-12_5s
measure diff play-12_5s
absent clientlog \bERROR\b

section seek-pause
do scriptevent geoui:seek 6
wait 1500
shot seek-6s
measure diff seek-6s
do scriptevent geoui:pause
wait 1500
shot paused-a
wait 1500
shot paused-b
measure diff paused-b
do scriptevent geoui:stop
wait 2000
shot stopped
measure diff stopped
absent clientlog \bERROR\b
