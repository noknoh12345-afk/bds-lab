# geoui_web_ws（bds/geoui/web/make.mjs が作る）: WebSocket 版を本物のアプリで（13 本目: 12 本目はつながらず — キーで打てたか・ゲームの答えを文字で見る、だめならチャットで）— プレイヤーが /connect 10.0.2.2:8787 を打つと、
# ゲームがサービスに WebSocket でつながり（暗号化はゲームが求めれば）、サービスが /web:pump でスクリプトとやりとりする。@minecraft/server-net なし。
# 実行: node lab.mjs app ci --batch geoui_web,geoui_web_ws（geoui_web は app-aspects.txt を scenario に、または単独）

section join
stop
launch
until title 600000
join
until joined 900000
size 2400x1080
wait 9000
do tp @a 0 -60 0 0 0
wait 3000
absent clientlog \bERROR\b

section type-key
key T
wait 2500
text /connect 10.0.2.2:8787
wait 1200
shot ws-typed-key
expect text (?i)connect

section send-key
key ENTER
wait 2500
shot ws-sent-key
until server GWEB ws linked 25000

section type-press
press T 80
wait 2500
press SLASH 60
press C 60
press O 60
press N 60
press N 60
press E 60
press C 60
press T 60
press SPACE 60
press 1 60
press 0 60
press PERIOD 60
press 0 60
press PERIOD 60
press 2 60
press PERIOD 60
press 2 60
press SHIFT+SEMICOLON 60
press 8 60
press 7 60
press 8 60
press 7 60
wait 1200
shot ws-typed-press
expect text (?i)connect

section send-press
press ENTER 80
wait 2500
shot ws-sent-press
until server GWEB ws linked 25000

section ocr-after
expect text (?i)(connect|websocket|wss?:|接続|unknown|不明|permission|権限|cheat|チート|syntax|構文|encrypt|暗号)

section open
shot world
do scriptevent gweb:open http://127.0.0.1:8787/demo/color.html?c=0000ff
until server GWEB page .*c=0000ff 60000
wait 9000
do scriptevent gweb:debug 1
wait 1500
shot ws-open
measure marks ws-open

section click
do scriptevent gweb:pointer 0.5 0.6
wait 1500
attack 0.3
until server GWEB page .*c=8000ff 20000
wait 3000
shot ws-click
measure marks ws-click

section site
do scriptevent gweb:go https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/Minecraft
until server GWEB page .*wikipedia 60000
wait 8000
shot ws-wiki
measure diff ws-wiki

section close
do scriptevent gweb:close
until server GWEB close 10000
wait 3000
shot ws-closed
measure diff ws-closed
